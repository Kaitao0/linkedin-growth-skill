# Scoring Rubric — LinkedIn Profile

Score each section from 0 to 100. Be strict. A "good enough" profile scores around 60. Only profiles that genuinely stand out cross 85.

For every section, report:
- Score
- 1–3 critical issues
- 1–3 quick wins

---

## Profile Photo (weight: 15%)

- **0–30**: missing, blurry, group photo, full body distant, party photo, sunglasses covering eyes, heavy filter.
- **31–60**: clear face but distracting background, casual attire mismatched with niche, harsh lighting, neutral expression that reads as cold.
- **61–80**: clean background, professional attire, eye contact, natural expression. Slight technical issues.
- **81–100**: studio-quality lighting, intentional background, attire and expression precisely calibrated to niche, face occupies 60% of frame, slight smile, sharp focus on eyes.

Specific checks:
- Eyes visible and in focus (no sunglasses).
- Face occupies ~60% of the frame.
- Background does not compete for attention.
- Attire matches the seniority and industry the user targets.
- Expression conveys approachability + competence (slight smile, no clenched jaw, no overly serious stare).

## Banner (weight: 8%)

- **0–30**: default LinkedIn gray.
- **31–60**: stock image with no message.
- **61–80**: branded image with the user's value proposition or niche keywords.
- **81–100**: custom banner with: headline of value proposition, contact/CTA, visual identity coherent with photo.

## Headline (weight: 15%)

A headline must do three jobs in 220 characters: (1) state who you are, (2) state what you do for whom, (3) include searchable keywords.

- **0–30**: only job title ("Coordenador").
- **31–60**: job title + company.
- **61–80**: role + niche + one differentiator.
- **81–100**: role + specific niche + measurable value + searchable keywords + light personality marker.

Anti-patterns to flag:
- Pipe-separated buzzword salad with no narrative.
- Vague generic adjectives ("passionate", "results-driven", "apaixonado por tecnologia").
- Mention of seeking opportunities at the start (use "Open to Work" badge instead).

## About (weight: 15%)

LinkedIn shows the first ~3 lines before "see more". The hook lives there.

- **0–30**: empty, or single sentence, or copy-paste of headline.
- **31–60**: paragraph describing role generically.
- **61–80**: clear hook + value proposition + relevant experience summary + CTA.
- **81–100**: hook in line 1 that makes the reader click "see more" + story arc + concrete results (numbers) + niche keywords naturally woven + clear CTA + contact info.

Structure to recommend:
1. Hook (1–2 lines, provocative or curiosity-driven).
2. Who you serve and what problem you solve.
3. Proof (1–3 results with numbers).
4. How you work / philosophy (1 short paragraph).
5. CTA (DM, email, calendar link).

## Featured (weight: 5%)

- **0–30**: empty.
- **31–60**: 1–2 generic links.
- **61–80**: 3+ items showcasing range (article, project, talk, certification).
- **81–100**: curated set with custom thumbnails and titles, each reinforcing the headline's positioning.

## Experience (weight: 12%)

For each role:
- **0–30**: only company + dates.
- **31–60**: bullet list of responsibilities.
- **61–80**: results with some numbers.
- **81–100**: each bullet follows action → context → result with numbers, includes keywords, mentions tools/technologies.

Flag any role with no bullets, or with bullets longer than 2 lines.

## Skills (weight: 7%)

- **0–30**: <5 skills or generic ones (Microsoft Office).
- **31–60**: 10–20 skills, mixed relevance.
- **61–80**: 25+ skills, all relevant to niche, top 3 pinned strategically.
- **81–100**: 50 skills, top 3 pinned to match headline keywords, multiple endorsements per skill.

## Recommendations (weight: 7%)

- **0–30**: zero received.
- **31–60**: 1–3 generic recommendations.
- **61–80**: 4+ recommendations from varied sources (managers, peers, clients).
- **81–100**: 8+ recommendations, recent (within 2 years), specific (mention concrete results), from senior people in the niche.

## Activity / Posts (weight: 10%)

- **0–30**: no posts in the last 90 days.
- **31–60**: occasional reshares, no original content.
- **61–80**: 1–2 original posts per month, low engagement (<20 reactions).
- **81–100**: weekly original content, mix of formats (text, carousel, video), engagement above niche baseline, comments quality high.

## Bilingual setup (weight: 6%)

- **0–30**: only one language; user targets international audience.
- **31–60**: EN partially filled (headline only).
- **61–80**: EN fully mirrored from PT, but literal translation.
- **81–100**: EN version culturally adapted (idioms, role titles localized to international standards), keywords adjusted to international recruiter searches.

If the user only targets local market, weight this section to 0 and redistribute weight to Activity.

---

## Overall calculation

```
overall = sum(section_score × section_weight) / sum(section_weight)
```

Always show the user:
- Overall score.
- Top 3 sections to fix first (highest weight × largest gap).
- Estimated effort per fix (low/medium/high).
